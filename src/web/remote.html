Speak instead of typing


Phone browsers only allow the microphone on secure (https) pages, and this page comes straight from your PS4, so the button cannot listen itself. Two ways to talk:


Keyboard microphone (easiest): tap the message box, then the microphone on the keyboard.

Android (Gboard): Gboard settings > Voice typing > on; the mic is at the top right of the keyboard.

Samsung keyboard: Settings > Samsung Keyboard > Voice input.

iPhone: Settings > General > Keyboard > Enable Dictation; the mic is at the bottom right.


Make this button work (Chrome on Android): open chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure, enter , set it to Enabled and restart Chrome.
